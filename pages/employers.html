Employees & HR Staff Are Struggling
to Find the Resources They Need

When employees’ financial needs are met, they’re empowered to be more productive and turnover is reduced by 30%.

A recent Morgan Stanley study demonstrates

  • Four out of five HR representatives say employees ask for help they can’t provide
  • 50% of HR staff and 40% of employees would like access to a financial advisor
  • 80% of HR representatives and 70% of employees have financial stress they feel affects performance and productivity
  • Of the 70% of employees who admitted to having financial stress, 45% say their primary challenge is paying off debt

According to a survey conducted by the Financial Health Network

  • 37% of employees report having medical debt
  • 32% of respondents say they had trouble paying medical bills in the last 12 months
  • Nearly 4 out of 5 employees with high financial stress say medical debt distracts them from work
  • 50% of workers who report debt as a source of stress say they spend at least one hour per week at work
    dealing with debt-related issues (such as contacting creditors)
