The Steps

Step 1

Call the healthcare provider for a bill with CPT codes.

An itemized bill with CPT codes shows each service you were charged for. Those codes are what you use next to check the Medicare rate and spot charges that do not belong.

Step 2

Do an internet or AI (i.e., ChatGPT) search for the Medicare rate for each CPT code.

Search the Medicare rate for every code on the bill. That rate is a reference for what the service usually costs, so you can see how far the bill sits above it before you negotiate.

Step 3

Set up a monthly payment plan that fits your budget.

Once you know a fair number, ask the billing office for a monthly plan you can actually keep. A plan that fits your budget is more useful than a lump sum you cannot pay.
